About

I'm Alper Sahistan(Şahıstan), a PhD student at SCI Institute at the University of Utah. I work on scientific visualization, volume rendering, and GPU-accelerated ray tracing. My master's thesis explored GPU-based data-parallel rendering for large unstructured volumes.

Further Information
  • Currently working on fun ray-tracing problems in scientific volume rendering, computational schlieren imaging, and point queries.
  • I have recieved my MS & BS degrees from Bilkent University Department of Computer Engineering
  • Interned at Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory in 2022 and 2025 as a Graduate Computing Scholar.
  • Worked under Intel Center of Excellence at University of Utah to develop a SYCL implementation of compression library ZFP.
